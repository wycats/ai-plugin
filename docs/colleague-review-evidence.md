# Evidence behind Colleague Review

22 October 2026. These encounters informed the canonical
[Colleague Review workflow](../skills/workflows/colleague-review/SKILL.md).
They record observed contributions and the reasons they shaped the workflow. The reviewers were agents acting as simulated colleagues. Their
reactions are useful development evidence; they are not human usability research
or endorsements by the design studios used as references.

The linked working records below are local development evidence. They are
retained in the originating workspaces and are not included in a clean clone.
The portable examples ship with the workflow in `examples.md`.

## Code and screen walkthroughs

The [ts-docs architecture walkthrough](/Users/wycats/Code/ts-docs/.local/reader-walkthrough/2026-10-15.md)
gave two fresh readers a common opening account, then stopped after conceptual
chunks for predictions and questions. Their responses determined the next
explanation. Existing architecture documents were withheld so the sampled code
had to support the account.

One reader qualified “promotion as one unit”: the implementation moves output
aside, installs the candidate, and attempts rollback on failure. Another found
that a render callback also commits source-cache entries and retains private
payloads. The exchange corrected the coordinator's explanation and identified
a hidden lifecycle contract. It did not justify redesigning the whole engine.
See lines 9–25 and 37–49 in that record.

The [screen repetition](/Users/wycats/Code/ts-docs/.local/docs-walkthrough/findings.md)
used actual saved browser pixels. Both readers understood the nested expansion
payoff, but could not tell whether Project was authored configuration or the
resolved internal model. Publication narration supplied motivation absent from
the page. These findings distinguish a successful guided explanation from an
artifact that carries the explanation itself. Source supplemented particular
screen questions; screenshots did not qualify keyboard or hover behavior.
See lines 3–27 and 50–58.

## Visual guide and transfer

The [two-reader review record](../.runtime/manuscript-font-rebuild/refinements/batch-three/visual-identity/review-record.html)
recovers the ts-docs method and its adaptation to the reading-site guide. A
newcomer and a practitioner received the same opening and specimens before the
guide. Questions about pigment meaning and penwork produced explicit distinctions
between Connection, Location, and Control.

After reading the guide, the practitioner proposed a Diagnostic Questioning
gloss that described hypothesis testing. Reading the canonical stance corrected
the commission and the guide's source-grounding guidance. Another reader caught
obsolete responsive screenshots. A later round used current captures and could
reconstruct disclosure, map, and return behavior while retaining the limits of
screen evidence.

The [completion review](../.runtime/manuscript-font-rebuild/refinements/batch-three/visual-identity/completion-review.html#review)
records questions about map destinations and narrow-note association. Its further
Observational Grounding case tested a new written commission; no resulting
illustration was generated. The [implementation review](../.runtime/manuscript-font-rebuild/refinements/batch-three/visual-identity/implementation-review.md)
separately records source confirmation of a return-origin gap and correction of
an overbroad guide claim. Its ongoing panel check at task transitions is a
user-requested arrangement for this site work, not a requirement for every review.

## A continuing architecture colleague

The [Recon/PER consultation](../.runtime/recon-per-authoring-consultation-2026-10-20.md)
with **Finish harness + v0 (architecture)** brought accumulated writing research
to a new set of drafts. The colleague connected rhetorical economy with the
context executing agents need, distinguished a worker's ability to reason about
the whole from authority to act, and explained why a preparer's reasons remain
useful when a prediction holds. It explicitly distinguished fresh context from
independent judgment.

The [subsequent reviews](../.runtime/recon-per-drafts/review.md) removed an
early requirement to know a detail's significance, allowed conversation to confirm
the approach, gave an example an unresolved moment, and made the reviewer's return
to the artifact explicit. A later pass found wording that made a formulated
question a prerequisite for seeking help. These changes came from an informed
colleague examining actual drafts over several exchanges. The consultation was
not an independent replication of the whole project.

## A fresh editorial encounter

The [README reader pilot](../.runtime/editorial-rethink/reader-review.md)
gave a fresh agent purpose, audience, and sources before the coordinator's
editorial rationale. It found a missing practical orientation and investigated
the Recon worker's source to clarify how the agent uses the workflow. Its question
about practical versus theoretical emphasis elicited an explicit editorial
choice. The coordinator incorporated the substantive proposal and shortened two
phrases. This supports another reading followed by discussion and reconciliation;
one use does not establish superiority over the previous linter.

The broader [evidence account](../.runtime/editorial-rethink/colleague-review-recon.md)
includes catalog, RFC, context-delivery, and experiment-review examples. Current
[Recon](../skills/workflows/recon/SKILL.md) and
[PER](../skills/workflows/per-cycle/SKILL.md) remain the owners of adaptive
investigation and review organization. Colleague Review captures the
encounter, discussion, and attempted application that connected those
relationships in the local work.
