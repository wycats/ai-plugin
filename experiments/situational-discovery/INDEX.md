# Finding an approach as the work develops

Start with the situation you recognize. The passages below offer several ways
into shared skills; follow the distinctions that become useful as you work.
Use ordinary tools directly when they already answer the request.

## Developing an explanation

When evidence must guide where you investigate next, [Recon](../../skills/workflows/recon/SKILL.md)
helps you follow it. As details change your understanding, [interpretive synthesis](../../skills/stances/interpretive-synthesis/SKILL.md)
helps you reconsider what earlier observations mean and which question is
worth pursuing next.
When observations appear incompatible, [observational grounding](../../skills/stances/observational-grounding/SKILL.md)
helps establish what each observation actually describes as the explanation develops.

Before trying a change, [hypothesis forming](../../skills/stances/hypothesis-forming/SKILL.md)
makes your expectations specific enough to test. Afterward,
[hypothesis evaluating](../../skills/stances/hypothesis-evaluating/SKILL.md)
examines what the difference between expectation and outcome reveals about
those assumptions.

## Finding what an account has lost

An explanation can become coherent while leaving something consequential
behind. [Gap-reading](../../skills/stances/gap-reading/SKILL.md) returns to the
underlying material to find what was omitted or never found a place. When the
question concerns unfinished work across a conversation,
[dangling-thread review](../../skills/workflows/dangling-thread-review/SKILL.md)
provides a way to examine it. What you recover may change the explanation;
[interpretive synthesis](../../skills/stances/interpretive-synthesis/SKILL.md)
continues that revision.

## Understanding something with another person

When a decision depends on another person's intent, priorities, or experience,
[diagnostic questioning](../../skills/stances/diagnostic-questioning/SKILL.md)
helps each answer inform the next question. When their own thinking is still
taking shape, [Socratic elicitation](../../skills/stances/socratic-elicitation/SKILL.md)
helps them examine and articulate it. Reading a shared artifact together can
make both perspectives more precise: [walkthrough](../../skills/workflows/walkthrough/SKILL.md)
gives that conversation a rhythm of reading, discussion, and reconsideration.

## Examining and changing code

[Code-review](../../skills/workflows/code-review/SKILL.md) examines whether a
proposed change fulfills its purpose and what its details imply for earlier
judgments. [Implementation quality](../../skills/workflows/implementation-quality/SKILL.md)
helps existing code express its concepts clearly. When accumulated mechanisms
obscure what experience has taught the system,
[architectural distillation](../../skills/workflows/architectural-distillation/SKILL.md)
recovers those lessons and derives a structure that expresses them. Any of
these can reveal a question that needs [investigation](../../skills/workflows/recon/SKILL.md)
or a shared [walkthrough](../../skills/workflows/walkthrough/SKILL.md).

## Making reasoning available to a reader

[Reader orientation](../../skills/stances/reader-orientation/SKILL.md) considers
what someone can understand where they encounter the work. When proposing a
design that others will assess and build on,
[public design reasoning](../../skills/stances/public-design-reasoning/SKILL.md)
connects its purpose, its workings, and the consequences of adopting it. When
writing in someone's name,
[authorial continuity](../../skills/stances/authorial-continuity/SKILL.md)
keeps their judgment and relationship with readers present. A pull request's
particular explanation is developed by
[PR description writing](../../skills/workflows/gh-write-pr-description/SKILL.md).

## Choosing what work to pursue

When several useful actions compete, [vision steering](../../skills/stances/vision-steering/SKILL.md)
relates them to the change the project exists to create. Across active chats,
[thread coordination](../../skills/workflows/thread-coordinator/SKILL.md)
helps maintain responsibilities and dependencies. An unresolved possibility
may need [gap-reading](../../skills/stances/gap-reading/SKILL.md) before it can
be considered alongside the work already underway.

## Looking up another capability

The [full catalog](CATALOG.md) lists every skill by its canonical name and
current description. Consult it when you know a name, need a capability these
passages do not expose, or find that a promising route has not resolved the
situation. Following a link makes its guidance available; the task and any
explicit invocation requirement still determine whether to apply it.
