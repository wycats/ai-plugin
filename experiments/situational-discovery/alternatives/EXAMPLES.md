# Finding an approach as the work develops

The work can change what kind of help is useful. These situations show several
approaches becoming relevant together. Follow the links when that guidance
would help with your task; ordinary tools may already answer it.

A bicycle mechanic hears that the rider is exhausted on a familiar hill.
Examining the drivetrain can test one explanation. A conversation may reveal
that the rider now carries a child, changing what an adequate repair would
accomplish. [Recon](../../../skills/workflows/recon/SKILL.md) follows what can be
inspected; [diagnostic questioning](../../../skills/stances/diagnostic-questioning/SKILL.md)
learns from the rider's experience. A [prediction](../../../skills/stances/hypothesis-forming/SKILL.md)
about the repair makes its assumptions testable. [Evaluating the result](../../../skills/stances/hypothesis-evaluating/SKILL.md)
can change both the diagnosis and which problem is worth pursuing.

An editor has shaped several interviews into a convincing story. Returning to
the notes reveals a remark that never fitted it. [Gap-reading](../../../skills/stances/gap-reading/SKILL.md)
recovers that remark; [interpretive synthesis](../../../skills/stances/interpretive-synthesis/SKILL.md)
examines how it changes the explanation and what earlier passages now mean.
The writer may recognize a concern they cannot yet articulate.
[Socratic elicitation](../../../skills/stances/socratic-elicitation/SKILL.md)
helps them explore it, while [authorial continuity](../../../skills/stances/authorial-continuity/SKILL.md)
keeps their judgment present as the draft changes.

An engineer sees three flags describing a resource's lifetime. A tidy enum
looks promising until a caller shows that ownership and temporary access can
coexist. [Implementation quality](../../../skills/workflows/implementation-quality/SKILL.md)
helps the code express the concepts, while [architectural distillation](../../../skills/workflows/architectural-distillation/SKILL.md)
can recover what earlier revisions learned about those relationships.
A [walkthrough](../../../skills/workflows/walkthrough/SKILL.md) lets a colleague's
knowledge reshape that account. [Reader orientation](../../../skills/stances/reader-orientation/SKILL.md)
asks whether the resulting code makes the distinction available where a caller
needs it.

The [catalog](../CATALOG.md) provides every approach by name and description,
including those these examples have not brought into view. Use it when another
need emerges or a familiar name would help. An example illustrates a
relationship; your task determines how far to follow it and any explicit
invocation requirement still applies.
