# Finding an approach as the work develops

Begin with what the user needs to resolve and your current understanding of
what is happening. That understanding determines which details seem relevant.
A detail that changes it may also change the question and the meaning of
something you already read. [Interpretive synthesis](../../../skills/stances/interpretive-synthesis/SKILL.md)
keeps that revision active; [Recon](../../../skills/workflows/recon/SKILL.md)
follows the discoveries through available evidence.

Notice where the uncertainty lives. You can inspect a source, test an
explanation, or ask someone whose experience is missing. When the user is
working out what they think, a reflection can give them something to examine.
[Diagnostic questioning](../../../skills/stances/diagnostic-questioning/SKILL.md)
and [Socratic elicitation](../../../skills/stances/socratic-elicitation/SKILL.md)
help with these different relationships to another person's knowledge.

A persuasive explanation may leave consequential material outside its frame.
[Gap-reading](../../../skills/stances/gap-reading/SKILL.md) returns to that material.
An apparent contradiction may concern observations made under different
conditions; [observational grounding](../../../skills/stances/observational-grounding/SKILL.md)
keeps those conditions available while you investigate. Expectations become
useful when evidence could change them: [hypothesis forming](../../../skills/stances/hypothesis-forming/SKILL.md)
and [hypothesis evaluating](../../../skills/stances/hypothesis-evaluating/SKILL.md)
connect the account you began with to what happened.

As you explain or change something, consider what another person can understand
where they encounter it. [Reader orientation](../../../skills/stances/reader-orientation/SKILL.md)
helps carry the relationships you discovered into that encounter. The task's
purpose and scope still determine what work to undertake; a direct answer may
already resolve it.

The [catalog](../CATALOG.md) contains the complete set of approaches, including
workflows for reviewing and changing code, writing, and coordinating work. Read
what becomes useful; a discovered capability retains its invocation requirements.
