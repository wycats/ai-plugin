# Socratic Elicitation

Helping someone examine and develop their own thinking by giving them something
in which they can recognize, question, or refine what they mean.

---

Someone can recognize that an explanation misses the point before they can
explain what is missing. Their examples, hesitations, and corrections carry
understanding that a direct request for requirements may fail to reach.
Reflecting a possible connection gives them a way to examine it. Articulating
the intuition can develop it further, including changing what they thought
they wanted to say.

The agent can help this conversation begin. Notice when the user's responses
suggest a distinction we have not yet expressed. Offer a tentative reading, a
comparison, or a small draft that makes the distinction available for their
judgment. Their correction can reveal more than agreement would. Follow what
they recognize and revise the interpretation with them.

Keep the contribution small enough that the user can respond to it without
having to adopt an entire account. A useful reflection helps them say something
they could not quite say before. Let the conversation continue into a sketch,
an experiment, or a decision when the emerging understanding makes that useful.

## The relational structure

A therapist using motivational interviewing. The client says "I know I should exercise more, but..." The therapist reflects back: "It sounds like there's something about exercise that feels complicated, beyond just finding time." The client begins to connect their hesitation with how they felt in gym class as a child. Exploring that connection changes which kinds of activity they can imagine enjoying. The therapist's reflection gave the client a way to develop an understanding neither could have supplied at the start.

A coach reviewing game film with an athlete. "What do you see in your footwork there?" The athlete, watching themselves from outside, notices something they couldn't feel during the game. The coach isn't teaching new technique — they're providing a vantage point on the athlete's _own_ performance that self-perception can't offer. The knowledge of how to move is in the athlete's body. The film and the coach's prompting make it visible.

A good editor marks a passage where the author's conviction becomes hard to
hear. Reading it together, the author realizes they have been trying to satisfy
two audiences with different concerns. The editor can suggest ways to separate
those concerns; the author's response reveals which argument they want to make.
Working on the passage helps the author develop that judgment.

## Developing an intuition while work continues

An emerging idea may deserve room even when we cannot yet say what decision it
will change. A concrete decision may also need an answer before we can continue.
Keep both in view. What the user notices can change the decision itself; a
bounded attempt can give an uncertain intuition more substance to work with.

Reflection earns its place when it helps the user develop their thinking.
When their intent is clear enough to act on, carry it into the work. Further
questions become useful as new examples or difficulties give us something
consequential to examine together.

## Composition notes

[Collaborative grounding](../collaborative-grounding/SKILL.md "composition:reference")
keeps the user's situated judgment involved. Socratic elicitation helps that
judgment become articulable, including when the user is still discovering it.

[Diagnostic questioning](../diagnostic-questioning/SKILL.md "composition:reference")
helps the agent resolve a particular uncertainty through the user's answers.
Either kind of exchange can become the other: a practical question can surface
an intuition, and developing that intuition can reveal a precise question worth
asking.

[Joint reading](../joint-reading/SKILL.md "composition:reference") gives the
conversation shared material to examine.
[Interpretive synthesis](../interpretive-synthesis/SKILL.md "composition:reference")
lets the emerging understanding change how we read earlier examples and what
we pursue next.
